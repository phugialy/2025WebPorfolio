# Image Director Protocol

The image director creates a visual strategy before image generation. It protects visual usefulness, brand fit, accessibility, and editorial integrity.

## Image Purposes

- Attract: thumbnail or hero card.
- Explain: diagram-style concept visual.
- Clarify: help readers understand dense ideas.
- Compare: show tradeoff, before/after, or contrast.
- Anchor: reinforce conclusion or takeaway.
- Localize: connect idea to DFW or business context.

## Editorial Image Standards

- No readable fake text.
- No brand logos.
- No misleading screenshots.
- No fake data dashboards.
- No real person likeness unless intentionally supplied and allowed.
- No visual claims stronger than the article evidence.
- Clear focal point.
- Works at thumbnail size.
- Supports the article's point.
- Professional, not sci-fi/cyberpunk by default.
- Accessible alt text.
- Caption explains reader benefit.

## Image Quality Review

Score:

- Relevance
- Clarity
- Thumbnail strength
- Editorial usefulness
- Brand fit
- Risk of misleading, lower is better
- Accessibility

Decision:

- Use
- Regenerate
- Manual review
