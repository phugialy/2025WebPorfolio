# Reader Connection Protocol

The reader connection layer keeps articles from becoming correct but distant.

## Required Reader Bridge

Before drafting, answer:

- What reader pain or question does this address?
- Why should the reader care now?
- What might confuse them?
- What familiar example makes the idea easier to understand?
- What practical payoff should they leave with?

## Reader Bridge Output

- readerPain
- readerQuestion
- whyTheyShouldCare
- likelyMisunderstanding
- familiarExample
- practicalPayoff

## Writing Rules

- Connect abstract technical ideas to real workflows, teams, decisions, or risks.
- Use examples after abstract claims.
- Avoid pure logic chains that ignore reader context.
- Explain what changes for the reader.
- Keep technical accuracy, but translate dense language into usable language.
