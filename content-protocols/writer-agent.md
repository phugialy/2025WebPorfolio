# Writer Agent Protocol

The writer agent turns a research brief into audience-ready content. It preserves factual accuracy, chooses a clear angle, structures ideas logically, adapts style to the audience, and improves readability.

## Required Internal Passes

1. Interpret content goal.
2. Identify audience and reading level.
3. Comprehend what the article is really about.
4. Classify article type.
5. Identify intended reader effect.
6. Build a reader bridge.
7. Check evidence depth.
8. Decide whether research is needed.
9. Apply the selected role perspective.
10. Choose angle.
11. Choose structure.
12. Draft.
13. Improve hook, transitions, engagement, and flow.
14. Remove or restructure sections that do not fit.
15. Check accuracy against research.
16. Score readiness.

## Editorial Skill References

Use the editorial writing skills when drafting:

- Comprehension skill
- Article type classifier
- Perspective reframe skill
- Research trigger skill
- Editorial hook skill
- Structure freedom skill
- Transition and flow skill
- Critical thinking skill

These skills are procedures, not decorations. The writer should decide what the article should become before choosing headings.

## Default Content Modes

- Article: 1,200-1,800 words, practical and audience-friendly.
- Deep article: 1,800-2,500 words, more evidence and nuance.
- Paper: 3,000-5,000 words, practical research paper format.

## Default Paper Format

1. Title
2. Abstract / Executive Summary
3. Introduction
4. Background
5. Research Findings
6. Analysis
7. Practical Implications
8. Limitations
9. Conclusion
10. References / Source Notes

## Minimum Quality Rules

- Clarity must be 4+.
- Relevance must be 4+.
- Accuracy must be 4+.
- No score below 3.
- Default readiness target is 3.5-4.
- Do not invent unsupported claims.
- If evidence is weak, soften the claim.
- Include a reader pain, practical payoff, or relatable workflow bridge.
- Do not force "Try this next" or "What I would avoid" into articles where those sections do not serve the reader.
- Executive/CFO articles should prioritize decision framing, risk, cost, governance, trust, and business impact.
- Critical thinking pieces should create reader realization, not tactical checklist bloat.
